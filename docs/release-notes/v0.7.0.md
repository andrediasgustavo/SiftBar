# SiftBar 0.7.0

This release makes the numbers easier to trust: leftovers now say how sure
SiftBar is, "System & other" can be opened up, and the whole report can be
exported.

- **Confidence for leftovers.** SiftBar now checks Homebrew Caskroom, Setapp,
  Spotlight, bundle IDs and installer receipts — not just `/Applications`.
  Each leftover is labelled **High**, **Medium** or **Low** confidence. Low
  rows may be shared vendor data and are review-only: no checkbox, no action.
- **A calmer Disk page.** The space map now opens compact: three numbers and
  four plain groups (Apps and tools, Your files, App data and caches, macOS and
  protected). **Show details** reveals the sub-buckets; commands, APFS volumes
  and purgeable space live behind **How SiftBar measured this**. Nothing
  technical is visible by default.
- **Larger, clearer type.** The sidebar, page titles, values and list rows are
  bigger. The old 10–11 pt captions were replaced by 13–14 pt text, and the
  advanced sections no longer open automatically.
- **Export the report as Markdown.** The new **Export** button (or `⌘E`) saves
  the current scan with findings, evidence, commands and confidence labels.
  Your home path is abbreviated to `~`. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- **Simulators and large app data are named.** Caches now shows iOS/iPadOS
  simulator storage, with a safe **Delete Unavailable** action and per-device
  deletion through Apple's `simctl` (only after confirmation). **iPhone device
  support** is a normal cache again. Large app data — Docker, OrbStack, UTM,
  Parallels, VMware, iPhone backups, Xcode archives and Steam — is listed
  read-only with **Show in Finder**, so you can see what is taking space
  without SiftBar deleting your data.
- **Clearer results.** The result card now separates space **freed
  immediately** (deleted simulators) from space **moved to the Trash** (caches
  and leftovers). Informational rows now sit at the bottom of every list,
  after the actionable Safe items, instead of mixing into the review queue.
- **Clearer leftover counts.** Low-confidence rows are no longer counted as
  findings or as reclaimable space. The Leftovers page also filters by
  confidence.
- **More honest disk accounting.** The drill-down skips categories that would
  double count, and leaves anything that does not fit in an explicit
  "Other macOS and protected data" row.
